Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This site is a set of static pages and one tool that runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no sign-up, no newsletter, no comment section and no advertising. Nothing you open, type or check here is sent to a server we control.

What this site does not do

  • No accounts and no forms. There is nothing to register for, and no field anywhere on this site that asks for your name, your email address or your file.
  • No advertising network. No third-party ad script is loaded on any page, so no advertising identifier is placed on you here.
  • No tracking pixels or social embeds. There are no embedded videos, no like buttons and no pixels from other companies.
  • No selling or sharing of anything. There is no customer list to sell, because there are no customers.

What is recorded when you visit

Two things, both of them ordinary web hosting traffic rather than anything this site asks for.

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics. The site is served by Cloudflare, which provides a privacy-first traffic measurement that records page views, the referring site, the country the request came from, and the type of device. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint the browser, and cannot follow you to another site. Because it stores no identifier for you, the resulting figures are aggregate counts — there is no per-visitor record to look up.
  • Standard server logs. Cloudflare logs requests (IP address, path, user agent, timestamp) as part of running and protecting the service. This is used to keep the site up and to deal with abuse, and it is retained under Cloudflare's own schedule rather than ours.

The file size checker

The file size checker reads your .pptx or .potx file inside the page itself. The file is opened by your browser, parsed by a script served from this site, and never uploaded — there is no endpoint for it to be sent to.

You do not have to take that on trust. Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network panel, and run a file through the checker: no request carries the file. Closing the tab clears whatever was read.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. The traffic measurement described above is cookieless by design, which is why there is no cookie banner on these pages.

Links to other sites

The guides link out to Microsoft's own documentation so that every instruction can be checked against its source. Those pages are Microsoft's, and what Microsoft records when you visit them is covered by Microsoft's own privacy statement — see Microsoft's privacy statement, which governs PowerPoint itself as well as its support pages. A link here is not an endorsement of anything on the other side of it.

Your choices

Because no identifier is stored, there is no profile to export, correct or delete. If you believe something on this site has nonetheless handled your data badly, write to the address below and say what you saw — that is a real route to a fix, not a formality.

Blocking the analytics script in your browser does not break anything here. Every page and the checker work without it.

Children

This site is written for people doing office work in PowerPoint. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone — which includes children.

Changes to this policy

If what the site collects ever changes, this page changes first and the effective date at the top moves with it. The date is not refreshed to look current; it is the date of the last substantive edit.

Contact

This site is run by Kai Jin, an independent web developer — the same person who writes the guides, described on the about page. Questions about this policy, or about anything else here, go to kaixinjin0205@gmail.com.

How these guides are written
